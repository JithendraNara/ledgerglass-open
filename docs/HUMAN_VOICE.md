# Human voice and design gate

The showcase should sound like the person who built it explaining the work aloud.

## Copy review

- Use first person for origin, mistakes, decisions, and lessons.
- Name the concrete problem before the architecture term.
- Keep a limitation or proof beside every important claim.
- Label every synthetic household and case as fictional.
- Do not invent users, testimonials, savings, adoption, or performance.
- Describe what a model does; do not use “AI-powered” as a substitute.
- Read the homepage and changed case studies aloud before publication.

The content validator rejects the project’s explicit generated-marketing phrase list.
That automated check is only a floor. A human still reviews cadence, specificity, and
whether a paragraph sounds like experience rather than a landing-page template.

## Visual review

The design uses warm paper, charcoal, muted rust and blue, local editorial type,
ledger lines, evidence stamps, and simple diagrams. It avoids generated illustration,
glowing gradients, glass panels, fake dashboards, stock finance imagery, and movement
that does not explain state. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion,
contrast, and responsive layouts are release checks.
